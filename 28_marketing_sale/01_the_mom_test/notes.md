## Avoid bad data

Three types of bad data
  * compliments
  * fluff (generics, hypotheticals, future)
  * ideas
  
### Compliments

The main source of compliment is seeking approoval, don't fish for them fish for truth
"The Pathos Problem"
Fix
  * keep the converstaion focued on the other person
  * ask about specific concrete cases examplee

Venture capitalis (professional judges of the future) are wrong far mot that right
You want facts adn commitments not compliments

How to start

**You guys seem to be donig a good job in this space - do you mind if I ask
how you're dealing with the stuff at the moment ?** 

You don't need to end up with what you wanted to hear in order to have a good converstaion. You just need to get to the truth.

## Asking important questions

Dont ask trivial questinos (Ex how old are you?)

Dont be affriaid to ask scary questions - you find them thought experiments
  * does answer changes your idea 
  * ask questions that has potential to destroy your idea
  * ask follow-up questions to understand the nature of their pain (how big it is)
    * is the problem not actully that big of a deal ?

Only market know if the idea/business is going to work. 
You are searching for the truth, not trying to be right
Do it as quickly and cheaply as possible

### Look before you zoom

Dont spend time on details. 
Sometime you have problems that you don't care to fix

Start broad and don't zoom until youve found a strong signal
